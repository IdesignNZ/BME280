The ESP32 stores up to 48 hours of one-minute averages in RAM. Resetting it, removing power, or uploading firmware clears the stored history. Hover or tap a graph to inspect an exact point. The live graphs are initially seeded from the last 10 minutes of RAM history. History zoom applies only to the stored graphs.
